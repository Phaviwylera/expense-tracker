YOU ARE IN CONTROL

Import & privacy.

Connect your SMS history

Enable Read SMS for historical scanning and Receive SMS for new alerts. Scans transaction SMS across banks. Known senders are labelled automatically; unfamiliar account/card alerts go to review so you can verify their bank.

How totals work

Only confirmed entries count. Transfers, excluded entries and review entries do not contribute to income or spending. Choose “Remember this merchant category” to apply your choice to matching past and future entries. Categories use merchant names found in the SMS. Unknown or ambiguous places stay Untagged. Select Untagged in Transactions, then choose a category to move an entry into Tagged.

SMS time is shown as the alert date. The actual payment date may differ. Unsupported message formats may not import.

Financial data is stored inside Android’s app storage. Online lookup sends only merchant names. Your SMS messages are never edited or deleted. Uninstalling the app removes its data.

Automatic merchant categories

Known brands are tagged on this phone. Searching Activity also checks unclear merchants online. Only a cleaned merchant name is sent; amounts, accounts and SMS text stay on your phone. Confident business matches tag remaining entries automatically. Manual categories are preserved.

Use Google Maps business lookup

Optional: enter your own Google Places API (New) key. Google requires API setup and billing, and requests may incur charges. Without a key, built-in merchant rules are used. Ambiguous matches stay Untagged.
